# Rackside

Rackside is a strength-training log for iPhone and Apple Watch. Build
routines, log every set from your wrist, and watch your progress.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email
**[racksideapp@gmail.com](mailto:racksideapp@gmail.com)** and I'll get back
to you.

**Restoring Rackside Unlimited:** the purchase belongs to your Apple Account.
On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open Settings in Rackside and tap
Restore Purchase.

**Moving to a new iPhone:** in Rackside, go to Settings → Backup → Export
Backup and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone, go to
Settings → Backup → Restore from Backup and choose that file.
